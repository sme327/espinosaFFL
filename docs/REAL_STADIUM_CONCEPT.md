# Real Stadium Concept Contract

Status: Approved by the family commissioner on 2026-08-23.

## Identity

Espinosa FFL Clubhouse is the family’s own football stadium: an authentic game-day destination made personal by five team crests, family history, and rooms that feel like places inside one venue.

The experience should feel exciting enough for a game-going football family, clear enough for a child who cannot read yet, and credible enough that the adults do not feel they are using a children’s app.

## Visual language

- Deep stadium navy is the dominant environment.
- Seahawks-inspired action green marks active choices, wayfinding, and important moments.
- Steel blue and concrete gray support structure without becoming a bright page background.
- Full team crests are the primary family imagery until approved photos exist.
- Scoreboards, section signs, field markings, tunnel lighting, championship banners, and locker nameplates define the rooms.
- Typography is condensed, direct, and legible, like stadium signage and broadcast graphics.
- Corners are square or minimally softened. Surfaces are architectural, not a collection of floating cards.
- Emoji may remain only where it carries meaning that has no finished visual replacement. It is not the identity system.

## Experience rules

1. Every page must read as a room in the same stadium.
2. Every primary destination must have a recognizable shape or image as well as a text label.
3. Full crests must remain readable at small sizes; no circular crops.
4. Action green is an accent, not a background wash.
5. White is reserved for high-contrast type and small details, never the main canvas.
6. One dominant visual moment per page is enough.
7. Decorative football elements must support orientation, history, or celebration.

## Room identities

- Clubhouse: stadium entrance and main concourse.
- Trophy Room: championship display and retired banners.
- Scrapbook: stadium program and season archive.
- Locker Room: player tunnel and five manager lockers.
- Rivalry Arena: primetime matchup presentation.
- Draft Room: draft stage, big board, and family war room.
- Weekly Fun: game-day prediction desk.
- Achievements: stadium ring of honor.

## Definition of success

Someone seeing the site without its title should describe it as a family football stadium—not a generic dashboard, scrapbook, or children’s game.
