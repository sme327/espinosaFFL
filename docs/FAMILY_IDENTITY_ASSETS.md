# Family Identity Asset Checklist

The clubhouse supports exactly five family members. Four are active league managers in 2026; Wyatt has a reserved future-manager identity and can be activated without changing the design.

## Identity fields

Each family member has these stable fields:

- Permanent manager ID
- Display name
- Season-specific team name
- Primary and light colors
- Emoji fallback
- Manager photo
- Team logo
- Optional short sound
- Optional pick celebration
- Optional draft interface mode: picture-assisted, guided, or strategy
- Active-league status, independent from family membership

Missing photos, logos, sounds, or interface modes deliberately fall back to the current emoji and colors. No placeholder portrait should be presented as a real family image.

## Image guidance

- Manager photo: square crop with the face large and recognizable at small sizes.
- Team logo: square transparent PNG preferred; it must remain readable at 48 pixels.
- Keep an original full-size copy outside the published site.
- Published images should use simple lowercase filenames tied to permanent manager IDs.
- Avoid embedding a season number in a reusable manager photo or logo filename.

## Approval checkpoint

Do not replace the emoji fallbacks until the family has approved the corresponding photo and team logo. Assets may be introduced one manager at a time without blocking the Draft Room foundation.
